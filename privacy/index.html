Privacy Policy

Credo for iPhone · November 7, 2026

In short: Credo collects nothing about you. There’s no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your progress stays on your iPhone and in the backups you choose.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress, settings, the texts you add, and your verses are stored in Credo’s own storage on your iPhone. If you turn on backups, Credo also writes them to the folder you pick; if that folder is in iCloud Drive, Apple stores it under your Apple Account and Apple’s privacy policy. Credo never sends your progress anywhere else.

Speak mode and hands-free review listen to your recitation to check it. Speech recognition runs on your iPhone; the audio is never recorded, kept, or sent anywhere.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

What Credo asks for over the internet

Songs
A song you open is downloaded from Credo’s file host (Cloudflare) and kept on your iPhone. Like any web server, the host sees your device’s IP address for the request. No account or identifier is sent.
The Bible
Chapters you read, verses you add, and words you search for in the Bible are fetched from Crossway’s ESV API (api.esv.org). Crossway receives the passage or words requested and your IP address, under Crossway’s privacy policy. Nothing else about you is sent.
Purchases
Songs and tips are bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles payment; Credo only learns, on your iPhone, which songs your Apple Account owns.

When you write to us

Report a Problem and Suggest an Idea open your own mail app with your Credo version, iOS version and iPhone model filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and what you send is used only to answer you and improve Credo.

Crash reports

If you’ve chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with Credo’s developer. Credo itself includes no analytics or crash-reporting code.

Children

Credo collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date. Questions: ben.robin@trinityriver.church.
