Credo

The creeds and catechisms, by heart.

Credo helps you memorize the great texts of the Christian faith, word for word: the Apostles’ and Nicene Creeds, the Heidelberg and Westminster Catechisms, the confessions, Scripture, or any text you paste in. It schedules each review for when you’re about to forget, and you can read, fill in blanks, type, listen or recite aloud.

No account, no ads, and nothing about you leaves your iPhone.

Need help, found a problem, or have an idea?
In Credo, open Settings → Report a Problem or Suggest an Idea; your version and iPhone model are filled in for you. Or write to ben.robin@trinityriver.church. I read every message.

Questions

Where is my progress kept?
On your iPhone. In Settings → Backups and progress you can back it up each day to a folder you choose (iCloud Drive works), export it, or import it on another iPhone.
I bought the songs on another iPhone. How do I get them back?
Settings → Songs and Purchases → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Purchases are shared with your Family Sharing group.
I have a code for the songs.
Settings → Songs and Purchases → Redeem a Code.
Speak mode doesn’t hear me.
Check that Credo may use the microphone and speech recognition in the iPhone’s Settings → Privacy & Security. Recognition runs on your iPhone; the first time, iOS may download its English speech model.
Can I start over?
Settings → Backups and progress → Erase all progress. Your current progress is backed up first.
